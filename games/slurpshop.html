Midnight Shift

Run a ramen shop through the night shift, 10 PM to 6 AM, with 1 to 8 friends. Cook, serve, scrub, restock, and keep the robber away from the register.

How to run the shop

  1. Take the order. Walk up to a seated customer and press the use key. Their order goes on the ticket rail (top left).
  2. Broth. Fill a pot with water, add a broth base from the red-tag crates on the island facing it, wait for it to boil. One pot makes 6 bowls.
  3. Noodles. Drop noodles in the boiler. Scoop them when they are ready, before they go mushy.
  4. Build the bowl. Grab a clean bowl, ladle broth, add noodles, then the toppings on the ticket from the topping bar.
  5. Serve it to the right seat. Faster and exact means bigger tips and better reviews.
  • Toppings need prep: boil eggs, chop pork and green onion on a cutting board, then carry them to their tray on the topping bar. Nori and corn go straight in.
  • Clear dirty bowls and wash them at the sink or you will run out. Mop puddles before someone slips.
  • Every ingredient sits in an open crate next to the station that uses it. Order more on the computer in the stockroom: the truck drops crates there, and you carry each one to its matching spot in the kitchen.
  • Before the shift, pick how busy it gets, how long customers wait and what is on the menu in the lounge. Some customers are trouble. Smack runners and robbers.
  • Rice bowls: cook rice in the rice cooker and the main (beef, chicken, cutlet or curry) in the wok, drop it on the matching tray, then scoop rice into an empty donburi from the stack by the trays and top it. Drinks: tea from the urn, soda and milk tea from the fountain, boba pearls from the pearl pot.
  • Events (turn them on or off in the lounge): kitchen fires (grab an extinguisher and hold Use on the flames), rush hour (a bus of customers), and power outages (flip the breaker in the stockroom).
  • The night shift runs from 10 PM to 6 AM (5 to 8 real minutes, set in the lounge). Watch the clock at the top. At 6 AM it is last call, then you get a receipt with the night's numbers and chef awards.
  • Stars matter. Every review pops up over the seat. Drop under 2 stars and the shop is shut down for good: money, upgrades and hats are gone.
  • Dishes: ramen and rice use the same bowls. Dirty bowls go in either sink (kitchen or dish station). Drinks need no cups.
  • Chef hats on the shop computer: faster running, faster chopping, longer throws, fire-proof, bigger tips.
  • Throw food and bowls (left click) across the kitchen: they land in the first tray, crate, pot or counter spot that takes them, and a teammate with empty hands can catch them.

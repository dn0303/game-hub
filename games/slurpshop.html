Slurp Shop

Run a late-night ramen shop with 1 to 8 friends. Cook, serve, scrub, restock, and keep the robber away from the register.

How to run the shop

  1. Take the order. Walk up to a seated customer and press the use key. Their order goes on the ticket rail (top left).
  2. Broth. Fill a pot with water, add a broth base from the storage room, wait for it to boil. One pot makes 6 bowls.
  3. Noodles. Drop noodles in the boiler. Scoop them when they are ready, before they go mushy.
  4. Build the bowl. Grab a clean bowl, ladle broth, add noodles, then the toppings on the ticket from the topping bar.
  5. Serve it to the right seat. Faster and exact means bigger tips and better reviews.
  • Toppings need prep: boil eggs, chop pork and green onion on a cutting board, then carry them to their tray on the topping bar. Nori and corn go straight in.
  • Clear dirty bowls and wash them at the sink or you will run out. Mop puddles before someone slips.
  • Order more ingredients and upgrades on the office computer. A truck drops the crates in the storage room: carry them to any shelf.
  • Every shift is a fresh run: you start with a little stock, $80 and nothing unlocked.
  • New broths and toppings unlock as you serve more people. Some customers are trouble. Smack runners and robbers.

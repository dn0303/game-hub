Blob Job

A squishy co-op shift for 1 to 8 friends. Haul the loot, hit the quota, do not get flattened.

How to play

  • Find clay treasures around the island and get them into Gulp, the big hungry pot, before the shift ends.
  • Bank enough to hit the quota and you move on to a harder day. Miss it and the whole crew is fired.
  • Heavy loot is slow to carry alone. Friends can grab the same thing to carry it together.
  • Fragile loot loses value every time it gets dropped or thrown hard. Set it down gently.
  • Rolly the rolling pin flattens you. A friend can pump you back up, or you pop back after a while.
  • Slink the puddle steals loot. Shove it to get your stuff back.
  • Puffcaps swell and pop when you get close and launch everything nearby.
